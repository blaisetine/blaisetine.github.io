Taught in the winter quarter. The course covers the fundamentals of modern processor design: instruction set architecture, pipelining and hazards, branch prediction, superscalar and out-of-order execution, caches and the memory hierarchy, multicore architecture, and power management, ending with GPUs, accelerators, and custom architectures. Students learn how the instruction set, the microarchitecture, and the technology trade off performance, power, and cost, and they implement and optimize a CPU pipeline simulator in C++.
