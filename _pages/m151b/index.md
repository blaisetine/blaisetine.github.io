How did Apple's M1 match competing laptop chips while using a fraction of the power? The answer spans the whole of computer architecture: the instruction set, the microarchitecture, the memory system, the process technology, and how they work together. This course covers the fundamentals of modern processor design: how processors execute programs, the design decisions behind them, and how to simulate and evaluate a processor.

We cover instruction set design, pipelining and hazards, branch prediction, superscalar and out-of-order execution, caches and the memory hierarchy, multicore architecture, and power management, ending with GPUs, accelerators, and custom architectures. In the projects, you implement and optimize a CPU pipeline in C++.

## What you will learn

- **The fundamentals of computer architecture.**
- **The design decisions behind a processor:** how the ISA, microarchitecture, and technology trade off performance, power, and cost.
- **How to simulate and evaluate a processor.**

## Textbook

David A. Patterson and John L. Hennessy, *Computer Organization and Design: The Hardware/Software Interface, RISC-V Edition*, Morgan Kaufmann. Additional materials are provided by the instructor.
