## First assignment: tell us about you

Tell us your school or department, your background and interests, and why you are taking this course. It earns a free bonus point.

## Homework (20%)

Ten multiple-choice homework assignments, worth 2 points each.

- Individual work.
- Released weekly and due the following week.

## Projects (35%)

Three projects on **CPU pipeline implementation and optimization**, written in C++. The first two are worth 10 points each and the last one 15.

- Each project runs for **two weeks**.
- Work in teams of two, or alone if you prefer. Teamwork is encouraged: most people learn better from peers, and understand better by explaining to someone else.
- Each partner submits separately, and partners must submit the same work.
- Knowledge of C++ is required.

## Exams (40%)

The midterm and the final are each worth 20%. Both are taken in class on a computer, with a review session beforehand.
