## Topics

1. Computer Model, Abstractions, and Technologies
2. Processor Design Metrics and Objectives
3. Instruction Set Architecture Design
4. Pipelining
5. Data and Control Hazards
6. Branch Prediction
7. Superscalar Processors and Out-of-Order Execution
8. Cache and Memory Hierarchy
9. Multicore Architecture
10. Power Management
11. Advanced Topics: GPUs, Accelerators, and Custom Architectures

## Exams

Both exams are taken in class. They are open book and test your understanding of the material rather than memorization.

## Policies

### Late submissions
Late work loses 10% of its grade. You have a 24-hour grace period to submit.

### Submission format
Submissions with a wrong file name or missing files lose 5%.

### Regrades
Request a regrade within one week of receiving your grade, with a written justification. A regrade request may lead to a full regrade of the assignment.

### Collaboration
Homework is individual work. Projects may be done alone or with one partner; each partner submits separately, and partners must submit the same work.
