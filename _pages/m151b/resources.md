## Textbook

David A. Patterson and John L. Hennessy, *Computer Organization and Design: The Hardware/Software Interface, RISC-V Edition*, Morgan Kaufmann.

## Further reading

- John L. Hennessy and David A. Patterson, *Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach*, Morgan Kaufmann. The graduate-level companion to the textbook.
- [The RISC-V Instruction Set Manual](https://riscv.org/technical/specifications/), the official ISA specifications.
- [AnandTech: Apple M1 and A14 deep dive](https://www.anandtech.com/show/16226/apple-silicon-m1-a14-deep-dive), the case study from the first lecture.
