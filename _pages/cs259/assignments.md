## First assignment: introduce yourself

Tell us your school or department, your background and interests, and why you are taking this course. It earns a free bonus point.

## Labs (25%)

Three labs on the [Vortex](https://github.com/vortexgpgpu/vortex) open-source GPU, worth 5, 10, and 10 points:

1. **Introduction to the GPU codebase**
2. **Ray casting**
3. **AI-based super sampling**

- Each lab runs for **two weeks**.
- You may work alone or with one partner.
- Released in weeks 1, 4, and 7.

Through the labs, you learn to:

- understand the GPU's software stack, compiler, and hardware microarchitecture;
- analyze performance;
- extend the instruction set, the compiler, and the hardware, in Verilog RTL or in the cycle-level simulator;
- carry out hardware/software co-design.

## Homework (10%)

Five homework assignments, released weekly in weeks 1 to 5.

## Paper critiques (10%)

Submit **five** critiques over the quarter, worth 2 points each.

- Individual work, due one week after release.
- Two pages covering the paper's motivation, contributions, key insights, results, and your critique.

## Paper presentation (10%)

Present a paper from the provided list in a **20-minute** talk, together with a partner. Presentations run from week 7 to week 10.

## Research project (20%)

Choose a topic from the provided list or propose your own. Teams of one to three, depending on the size of the project.
